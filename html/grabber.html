Grabber wanted to refresh their promotional brochures for both new and classic products with a streamlined, templated approach. Template products included fasteners (screws), caulk, framing collars, tools for fabrication, specialty fasteners, drywall assembly, and Grabber's proprietary SuperDrive line. Additional brochures included product training materials, corner & trim products, trash/debris carts, long-form catalog's and more! The solution was a modern clean and readable format that helped customers learn about new and existing products.
